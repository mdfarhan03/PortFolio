The Initiative
V2I-Based
Safety System
Digital Safety Signage & Speed Alert System. A smart city initiative to deliver dynamic rules and advanced alerts directly to drivers, ensuring safety regardless of external conditions.
The Problem
Poor
Visibility
Physical signs become invisible when conditions aren't perfect. Fog, heavy weather, physical blockages, and night driving lead to missed crucial information.
The Problem
High
Maintena nce
Maintaining physical infrastructure is expensive and creates continuous waste. Wear and tear, labor costs, and constant replacement drain city budgets.
The Problem
Human
Error
Speeding tickets and accidents happen simply because the driver "didn't see the sign." Distractions like phone and GPS use lead to severe sign blindness.
The Problem
Sensitive
Zones
Drivers often brake too late or ignore static signs in critical areas like School Zones and Hospital Zones, resulting in delayed reactions where seconds matter most.
The Solution
Wireless
In-Dash Alerts
Utilizing all-weather RF Technology to send alerts directly to the dashboard. The driver knows the speed limit instantly, even in absolute zero visibility.
The Solution
Digital
Infrastruc- ture
Instant remote updates via a central server. Zero physical maintenance is required on the road, saving millions in long-term smart city management.
The Solution
Audio-Visual
Interrupts
Active buzzer alerts and color-coded LEDs grab attention instantly. It is physically impossible to miss the warning, even if the driver is not looking at the road.
The Solution
Smart
Zones
Early digital warnings provide drivers with pre-warning alerts before they even reach the zone. Time-based rules automatically adjust limits during school hours.
CURRENT ACHEIVEMENT
PROTOTYPE
We have built a fully functional V2I prototype demonstrating real-time localized alerts. The hardware utilizes an Arduino Uno, NRF24 transceiver modules, an LCD display, and LEDs. Crucially, we successfully developed and implemented the core directional logic algorithm that ensures accurate sign targeting.
Next Steps
THE FUTURE
Our next phase involves developing a cloud-based central server for dynamic, city-wide updates. We will miniaturize the hardware into a premium, compact device that seamlessly integrates into any car dashboard. Furthermore, we plan to expand this critical safety technology by designing specialized units for motorcycles.
